Introduction

The Department of Computer Science provides students with a strong foundation in programming, software development, databases, computer systems, and modern computing technologies. The department encourages students to develop practical skills along with theoretical knowledge.

Degree Programs

  • Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS)
  • Bachelor of Software Engineering (BSSE)
  • Master of Science in Computer Science (MSCS)
  • PhD in Computer Science

Semester Courses

  1. Web Engineering
  2. Database Systems
  3. Computer Organization and Assembly Language
  4. Operating Systems
  5. Software Engineering

The ordered list starts from 3 and uses alphabetic numbering as required.
